About this system

The university holds student elections every year, and counting paper ballots by hand is slow and open to mistakes. This system moves the whole process online: the electoral officer creates an election, adds the positions up for grabs, and registers the students who are standing as candidates.

Eligible students register as voters for an election. Once the election is started, each voter casts a ballot, choosing one candidate for each position. The system checks that the ballot is valid and accepts only one ballot per voter for each position.

When the election ends, the votes are counted automatically and the results are ready to publish. Use the menu to manage students, elections, positions, candidates and voters, or to cast your ballot.
